What the Policy Desk is

The Policy Desk is where NACCL shares updates on federal issues important to member CoCs. As a 501(c)(6) professional association, NACCL has the flexibility to advocate and lobby federally on issues its members identify — and the Policy Desk keeps members informed along the way.
