Where things stand

The founding membership window closed on July 31, 2026. One hundred forty-nine Continuums of Care joined as founding members, from 42 states and the District of Columbia, covering all ten HUD regions and every CoC type. NACCL is incorporated as a nonprofit corporation and in good standing. Recognition by the Internal Revenue Service as a 501(c)(6) is a separate step and is underway. NACCL continues to operate while that is under review, invoicing founding dues and moving toward the first board election.

Why NACCL exists

CoCs did not have a national professional association of their own, so CoC leaders built one. More than 700 people from 43 states helped shape the governance structure, dues framework, and initial priorities. Members will elect the board and continue setting NACCL's direction.

More ways to learn from peers

CoC leaders told us they need more regular ways to connect with people doing the same work. New leaders often learn the job as they go, and what works in one community does not always reach another.

My onboarding was someone literally dropping a paper copy of the interim rule on my desk.

CoC lead agency director, March 2026 strategy session

Keeping knowledge in the field

When experienced people leave, CoCs can lose history and practical knowledge with them. Leaders talked about preserving what the field has learned and making it easier for the next person to pick up the work.

We need to capture some of the knowledge about CoC history and what has happened because so much of it gets lost when people leave this work.

Tamera Kohler, CA-601

Resources CoCs can own and maintain

CoC leaders also want resources that belong to the field and stay available over time: practical tools, shared information, and materials CoCs can maintain together.

We’ve gotten accustomed to having good resources on HUD Exchange and saw how easily that was washed. Having ownership over our own data and resources, the power behind that is huge.

Kenzie Strubank, KY-500

What happens next

  • Now

    Founding dues invoices

    Founding dues invoices go out to all 149 founding members, at the tier and amount recorded when the CoC pledged.

  • This fall

    Board nominations open

    Nominations open for the first elected Board of Directors. Members in good standing may nominate, be nominated, and vote.

  • Fall or winter

    First board seated

    The election is completed and the first Board of Directors is seated. The current officers hand off at that point.

  • December 8

    National convening in San Diego

    National convening in San Diego, California, at the Hilton San Diego Bayfront. The first gathering of the membership under an elected board.

A national association built specifically for CoCs

CoCs already work with state, regional, and national organizations across homelessness, housing, local government, and related fields. NACCL is not intended to replace that work. It is a different kind of organization: a professional association whose members are CoCs, governed by CoC leaders, and focused on the work of leading and coordinating a Continuum of Care.

That gives CoCs a place to learn from one another, develop leaders, build shared resources, and bring a common CoC perspective into federal conversations when members choose to. NACCL is being built for the long term, not tied to one federal proposal, administration, or funding decision.
