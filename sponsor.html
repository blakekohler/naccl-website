Sponsor the Convening

Partner with the CoC leaders shaping local homelessness systems — at the NACCL Annual National Convening, December 8, 2026, in San Diego.

Partner with us, not just sponsor us

On December 8 in San Diego, Continuum of Care leaders from across the country gather for the NACCL Annual National Convening around three purposes: a stronger profession, a trusted peer community, and a national voice. We invite your organization to help make it possible — as a partner, not just a sponsor.

Connect with decision-makers helping shape local homelessness systems. Learn what they need. Share what your organization makes possible. And begin relationships that can grow well beyond a single day in San Diego.

This is more than an opportunity to be seen. It is an opportunity to become known — and to help something important take shape.

More ways to connect

Purchase individually or add to a sponsorship level. Each includes a linked registration-page listing, sponsor-slide recognition, and a post-event thank-you. Level benefits are separate.

Exhibit Table

$3,000

Connect with CoC leaders in person. Includes one tabletop, exhibit access for up to two representatives during arrival and scheduled breaks, and an exhibitor listing in the digital agenda. Subject to venue capacity; convening sessions and Leaders Access are not included.

Recorded Sponsor Message

$2,000

Share one 30-second recorded message introducing what your organization does, played during the convening. Sponsor supplies the finished recording in advance; content and playback timing are subject to NACCL approval.

Leaders Access

$1,000

Receive an attendee list with professional contact information for attendees who opt in to sponsor outreach — names, titles, organizations, and work email addresses, as provided. For the purchasing organization's direct follow-up only; no resale or sharing with third parties.

Good to know

NACCL is a peer-led gathering, supported by partners. NACCL retains agenda and programming decisions. Sponsorship does not confer membership, voting rights, or preferred-vendor status. Convening registration and the additional opportunities above are separate unless expressly included.

Make the most of your San Diego visit: the RTFH Conference on Homelessness follows December 9–10 at the same hotel.
