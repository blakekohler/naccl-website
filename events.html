Events and Convenings

Opportunities to connect, strategize, and strengthen the shared capacity of the CoC Network.

National convening — December 8

San Diego, California · Hilton San Diego Bayfront · In person

The national convening is the first gathering of the membership under an elected board, and where the first-year priorities get set. It follows the fall board nominations and the seating of the first Board of Directors.

Get updates on registration

Leading up to the convening

  • This fall

    Board nominations open

    Nominations open for the first elected Board of Directors. Members in good standing may nominate, be nominated, and vote.

  • Fall or winter

    First board seated

    The election is completed and the first Board of Directors is seated. The interim officers hand off at that point.

  • December 8

    National convening in San Diego, California

    The first gathering of the membership under an elected board.

Peer learning, year round

NACCL's work is professional development, peer learning, shared resources, and support for the people who lead and coordinate Continuums of Care. Regular ways for members to connect will be announced as the first-year work plan takes shape.
