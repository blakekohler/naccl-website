Advocacy and Narrative

Tools, messaging frameworks, and examples that support CoC leaders in telling a unified, data-informed story.

A collective voice, when members choose to use it

As a 501(c)(6) professional association, NACCL has more flexibility to advocate and lobby federally on issues important to member CoCs than many member organizations have on their own. That means NACCL can take positions on federal issues identified by its members without asking any one CoC or lead agency to take that on.

Strategy and role

Translate field practice into policy

Collect input from CoC leaders and convert it into clear policy recommendations.

Shape the narrative

Build cross-sector alignment

Equip leaders to engage chambers, local government, and philanthropy.

Center outcomes and data

Promote transparent performance and continuous improvement.
